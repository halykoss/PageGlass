Mountains

Glass that only blurs looks like fog. Liquid glass keeps a little of what is behind it, bends it near the edges and catches a highlight along the rim. That is what the toolbars above are doing with this page, live, while you scroll.

Controls sit on top as frosted capsules: more blur, milkier, so their icons stay readable over any colour. The bar underneath stays glossy and clearer, and reflects the content right next to it.
